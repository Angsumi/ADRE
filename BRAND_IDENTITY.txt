1. BRAND STRATEGY & VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AxomRank is Assam's flagship competitive exam-intelligence platform designed 
specifically for the Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE 3.0 Grade 3 & Grade 4).

Core Positioning Pillars:
  1. Heritage Authority: Grounded in Assam's iconic cultural identity (Rang Ghar).
  2. Uncompromising Clarity: High-contrast, non-glare editorial typography for 
     prolonged study sessions.
  3. Programmatic Precision: Topic-wise PYQ solvers, real-time OMR rank analytics, 
     and structured syllabus hierarchies.

Target Audience:
  - 100,000+ ADRE Grade 3 & Grade 4 Aspirants across Assam
  - Students preparing in English and Assamese mediums
  - Mobile & desktop users requiring fast, high-contrast, clutter-free UI

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BRAND COLOR SYSTEM & DESIGN TOKENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The visual color system is directly extracted from the high-resolution golden-hour 
photography of the historic Rang Ghar monument in Sibsagar, Assam.

Primary Colors:
  • Warm Linen Sand (Primary Background):
    - Hex: #F9F4EB
    - RGB: rgb(249, 244, 235)
    - Usage: Full page canvas background, high-yield body container backdrop.
    - Rationale: Warm, non-glare natural paper tone that prevents eye strain.

  • Royal Ahom Terracotta Crimson (Primary Action Accent):
    - Hex: #9E3A26
    - RGB: rgb(158, 58, 38)
    - Usage: Primary CTAs, active tab toggles, key badges, high-priority highlights.
    - Rationale: Represents historic Rang Ghar terracotta brickwork & Ahom bravery.

  • Deep Espresso Black (Primary Typography & Structure):
    - Hex: #1D1814
    - RGB: rgb(29, 24, 20)
    - Usage: H1-H6 headings, primary body text, active state borders.
    - Rationale: 100% contrast readability; softer than pitch black for editorial aesthetic.

Secondary & Accent Colors:
  • Sunset Sky Heritage Gold (Badge & Accent Accent):
    - Hex: #C9A468 / Dark Accent: #A88244
    - Usage: Featured cards, PYQ tags, percentile rank stats, glow overlays.
    - Rationale: Extracted from golden hour sunshine light falling on the Rang Ghar roof dome.

  • Warm Oat Card Surface (Card Containers):
    - Hex: #F3EDE2 / Border: #E5DAC8
    - Usage: Bento cards, mock-test tier selector blocks, table rows.
    - Rationale: Tactile paper card surface with subtle border division.
