# 1. Executive Strategy

AxomRank should not position itself as another online coaching institute.

The stronger positioning is:

> **AxomRank is an exam-intelligence platform built specifically for Assam aspirants.**

The product should combine:

- syllabus mapping
- previous-year-question analysis
- high-quality MCQs
- realistic mock examinations
- performance analytics
- weak-topic identification
- revision systems
- Assam-specific GK
- current affairs
- personalized preparation
- bilingual Assamese/English experience
- AI-assisted study guidance

The product should make the student feel:

> “I know exactly what I should study today, why I should study it, and whether I am actually improving.”

This differentiates the brand from ordinary PDF sellers and generic coaching platforms.

---

# 2. Market Reality

The Assam ADRE market is already commercially active.

Current competitors include:

- coaching platforms selling ADRE courses around ₹699–₹1,999;
- dedicated mock-test platforms;
- study-material sellers;
- larger national platforms;
- AI-assisted preparation products.

For example, Lucent currently offers ADRE courses from approximately ₹699 to ₹1,999, including mocks, classes and PYQs.

GyanTest promotes an ADRE mock-test product with 50+ full-length tests and detailed explanations.

Sadhna is positioning itself around AI-powered study planning, mock analysis and personalized preparation, reportedly targeting ADRE aspirants across Assam's districts.

AssamTest competes with a very large question-bank/mock-test proposition.

Therefore:

**Do not compete primarily on “more questions.”**

Compete on:

> **better preparation intelligence + better Assam-specific experience + better UX.**

# 6. The Killer Feature: Mistake Bank

This should become an AxomRank signature feature.

Every wrong question automatically enters:

> **My Mistakes**

The system remembers:

- question
- topic
- reason for failure
- date
- previous attempts
- subsequent attempts

Then periodically asks the student again.

The psychological message:

> **Your mistakes don't disappear. They become your preparation.**

This creates retention.

# 17. Pricing Philosophy

Do not attempt to beat everyone by being the cheapest.

Instead:

> **Make the free product unusually good, and make the paid product obviously more useful.**

Current Assam products demonstrate that consumers are already seeing ADRE preparation offers ranging from low-cost mock series to ₹1,000–₹3,000+ courses.

Your advantage as a digital-first product can be:

**low fixed cost + high product quality + inexpensive pricing.**

# 21. Trust Strategy

This category is extremely important.

Never fake:

- student numbers
- ranks
- selections
- testimonials
- government association
- “official” status

Explicitly state:

> **AxomRank is an independent educational platform and is not affiliated with the Government of Assam, SLRC or any examination authority.**

Official exam information should always link back to the government source.

The Assam government maintains official recruitment/legal information for the direct-recruitment system, so your product should distinguish clearly between educational guidance and official information.

# 29. Competitive Moat

Your moat should become the data.

Every interaction produces information:

```text
Question
↓
Attempt
↓
Correct/Wrong
↓
Time
↓
Topic
↓
Difficulty
↓
Student profile
↓
Weakness
↓
Personalized revision
```

After tens of thousands of attempts, you can discover:

- hardest topics
- most-missed questions
- average solving time
- misleading question patterns
- high-yield topics
- student weaknesses
- score distributions

That becomes your proprietary **Assam Exam Intelligence Dataset**.

This is much harder to copy than a website.

# 32. Immediate Execution Order

Do **not** start by building the whole platform.

Do this in order:

**1. Finalize the brand name.**

**2. Check domain + social handles + Indian trademark availability.**

**3. Create the visual identity.**

**4. Define the ADRE question database schema.**

**5. Build 500 excellent questions.**

**6. Build one excellent mock test.**

**7. Build the result/analytics screen.**

**8. Put it in front of 20 real ADRE aspirants.**

**9. Watch what they actually do.**

**10. Only then build the full platform.**

The biggest mistake would be spending two months building a sophisticated platform before proving that students actually want your particular preparation experience.
