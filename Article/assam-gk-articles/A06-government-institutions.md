## The Six Autonomous Councils

Assam's most distinctive institutional feature is its **six autonomous councils**, which give its tribal communities self-governance under the **Sixth Schedule and Article 244** [1] [2]. The **Bodoland Territorial Council** (Kokrajhar) governs the four-district BTR with legislative and executive powers under the 2020 Accord; the **Karbi Anglong Autonomous Council** (Diphu) and **Dima Hasao Autonomous Council** (Haflong) administer the two hill districts; and the **Rabha Hasong** (Dudhnoi), **Tiwa** (Morigaon), and **Deuri** (Sivasagar) councils serve their respective communities with more limited administrative powers [1] [2]. Together they make Assam the state with the largest number of autonomous councils in India.

## The Executive and the City

The **Government of Assam Secretariat** at **Dispur** — a neighbourhood within Guwahati and **India's smallest state capital** — houses all departments and the Chief Minister's office [1] [2]. Urban governance centres on the **Guwahati Metropolitan Development Authority (GMDA)** and the **Guwahati Municipal Corporation** (the only municipal corporation in the state), supported by eight municipal boards [1] [2]. **ASSAMPOL** — headquartered at Ulubari — administers one of the country's larger state police forces, with specialised units for border security, counter-insurgency, and the Brahmaputra's riverine beats [1].

## Education and Research Institutions

Assam's institutional weight also includes national-grade centres: **IIT Guwahati**, **Gauhati University** (1948), **Dibrugarh University** (1965), **Cotton University** (2017 — the state's first fully state-funded university), the **Tezpur University** (central), and research institutions like **Tocklai** (tea), the **Regional Research Laboratory** (now IICT Guwahati), and the **Assam Agricultural University** [1] [2]. The **Assam Public Service Commission (APSC)** — the gateway to Grade 3 and Grade 4 recruitment itself — remains the most consequential institution for every ADRE candidate [1].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many autonomous councils does Assam have?

Six: Bodoland Territorial Council, Karbi Anglong AC, Dima Hasao AC, Rabha Hasong AC, Tiwa AC, and Deuri AC — the most of any Indian state [1] [2].

### Where is the Assam government secretariat located?

Dispur, a locality within Guwahati — India's smallest state capital [1] [2].

### What is the only municipal corporation in Assam?

The Guwahati Municipal Corporation, supported by eight municipal boards across the state [1] [2].

### Which council governs the Bodo community?

The Bodoland Territorial Council at Kokrajhar, operating under the Sixth Schedule and the 2020 Bodoland Accord [1] [2].

### What is ASSAMPOL?

The Assam Police headquarters at Ulubari, Guwahati — administering the state's police force across 36 district jurisdictions [1] [2].

### Which body conducts ADRE recruitment?

The Assam Public Service Commission (APSC) — the state's public service commission for Grade 3, Grade 4, and other recruitments [1].
