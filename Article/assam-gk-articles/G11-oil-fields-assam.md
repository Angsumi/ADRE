## The Digboi Story: Asia's First Oil

The legend runs that an English engineer, observing elephants emerging from the jungle with oil-slicked feet at **Digboi**, recognised the seepage and ordered a well — leading to Asia's first commercial production [1] [2]. The well at Digboi began producing in **1889** (the first exploratory drilling dated 1866–67), and the refinery commissioned in **1901** remains the **oldest working refinery in the world**, still operating under Indian Oil Corporation [1] [2]. The town's name itself is said to derive from the Assamese instruction "dig, boy, dig!" — a folk etymology the Oil Museum at Digboi happily celebrates [2].

## The Field Map: Upper Assam's Oil Belt

The producing fields cluster in upper Assam's Tinsukia–Dibrugarh–Sivasagar belt [1] [2]: **Digboi** (the pioneer field), **Naharkatia** (discovered 1953, a major producer), **Moran** (discovered 1953–56, one of the largest), **Rudrasagar-Lakwa** (the "Rudra Sagar" field of Sivasagar), **Geleky**, and **Charaideo** — plus newer discoveries in the region. **Oil India Limited**, headquartered at **Duliajan** (Tinsukia), is the state-owned explorer that has operated Assam's fields for decades and is today a Navaratna CPSE [1]. The **Naharkatia-Moran** corridor and the Digboi field remain the historic heartland of India's onshore production.

## Refineries and the Petrochemical Web

Assam hosts four refinery sites in its modern history: **Digboi** (1901), **Guwahati** (1962 — India's first public sector refinery, at Noonmati), **Numaligarh** (1999, with its recent expansion and the Northeast's pipeline grid), and **Bongaigaon** (closed as a refinery and converted to a terminal) [1] [2]. Together they feed the state's fertilizer, petrochemical, and LPG infrastructure — and the promised **No. 7 refinery at Numaligarh** was one of the undertakings of the Assam Accord era [2].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where was Asia's first commercial oil well drilled?

At Digboi in upper Assam — first drilled in 1866–67 and producing commercially from 1889 [1] [2].

### Which is the world's oldest working oil refinery?

The Digboi refinery in Assam, commissioned in 1901 and still operating under Indian Oil Corporation [1] [2].

### What are the major oil fields of Assam?

Digboi, Naharkatia, Moran, Rudrasagar-Lakwa, Geleky, and Charaideo — clustered in the Tinsukia–Dibrugarh–Sivasagar belt [1] [2].

### Where is Oil India Limited headquartered?

Duliajan, in Tinsukia district — Oil India is a Navaratna central public sector enterprise [1].

### Which was India's first public sector refinery?

The Guwahati (Noonmati) refinery, commissioned in 1962 [1] [2].

### How much of India's crude does Assam produce?

Roughly 12–16% depending on the year — among the largest onshore producing regions of the country [1].
