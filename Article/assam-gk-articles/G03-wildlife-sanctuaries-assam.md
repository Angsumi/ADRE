Beyond its seven national parks, Assam protects its biodiversity through a network of **wildlife sanctuaries** — roughly eighteen established reserves plus community reserves and deer parks — that guard everything from the world's densest rhino population to the only habitat of the endangered **hoolock gibbon** and **golden langur** [1] [2]. Sanctuary questions in ADRE hinge on superlatives: the oldest, the smallest, the densest, and the species-restricted reserves. This guide ranks them exactly the way the exam asks.

## The Gibbon, the Rhino, and the Langurs

Assam's species-restricted sanctuaries are its most exam-friendly. **Hoollongapar Gibbon Wildlife Sanctuary** (Jorhat, established 1997) is the **only sanctuary in India exclusively for the hoolock gibbon** — India's only native ape, found nowhere else in the country [1] [2]. **Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary** (Morigaon, ~16 sq km) packs the **highest rhino density in the world**, out-densifying even Kaziranga on a per-hectare basis — a fact ADRE tests almost every year [1]. The **golden langur**, endemic to a small strip along the Bhutan frontier, is protected at **Chakrasila** (Kokrajhar–Dhubri) and **Bornadi** (Udalguri), with Bornadi additionally hosting the **pygmy hog** and **hispid hare** — two of the world's rarest mammals [1] [2].

## The Oldest and the Smallest

**Garampani Wildlife Sanctuary** in Karbi Anglong, notified in **1952**, is counted among the oldest sanctuaries of Assam and of India; despite covering barely six square kilometres, it shelters hot water springs, waterfalls, orchids, and rare birds near the Nambor reserve forests [1] [2]. At the other end of size, Pobitora's 16 sq km makes it one of the smallest — yet the densest — rhino habitat on Earth [1].

## Wetland and River Sanctuaries

**Deepor Beel** (Kamrup), a permanent freshwater lake on Guwahati's southwestern edge, is Assam's flagship **Ramsar site** (designated 2002) and an Important Bird Area where wintering waterfowl gather in the thousands [1] [2]. **Pani Dihing** (Sivasagar), **Bura Chapori** and **Laokhowa** (Sonitpur, on the Brahmaputra's north bank), **Nambor** and **Nambor Doigurung** (Golaghat, with orchids and primates), **Sonai Rupai** (Sonitpur), **Marat Longri** (Karbi Anglong), **Amchang** (Kamrup), **Rowta** (Udalguri), **Bhairabkund** (Baksa), **Dibru-Saikhowa**, and **Panbari** (Dhubri, golden langur) complete the official network maintained by the Forest Department [1] [3].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the oldest wildlife sanctuary in Assam?

Garampani Wildlife Sanctuary in Karbi Anglong, notified in 1952 — among the oldest sanctuaries in India [1] [2].

### Which sanctuary is dedicated to the hoolock gibbon?

Hoollongapar Gibbon Wildlife Sanctuary in Jorhat district — the only sanctuary in India exclusively for the species [1] [2].

### Which sanctuary has the highest rhino density in the world?

Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary in Morigaon district, at barely 16 sq km [1].

### Where is the golden langur protected in Assam?

Mainly at Chakrasila (Kokrajhar–Dhubri), Bornadi (Udalguri), and Panbari (Dhubri) sanctuaries along the Bhutan border [1] [2].

### What is Deepor Beel famous for?

It is Assam's Ramsar wetland (2002) — a permanent freshwater lake near Guwahati and an Important Bird Area [1] [2].

### How many wildlife sanctuaries does Assam have?

About eighteen established sanctuaries, besides seven national parks, two community reserves, and deer parks, as per the official protected-area network [1] [3].
