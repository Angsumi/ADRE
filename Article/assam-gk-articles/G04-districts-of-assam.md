Assam is administered through **35 districts** organised into **five divisions** — Upper Assam, Middle Assam, Lower Assam, the Bodoland Territorial Region (BTR), and the Barak Valley [1] [2]. District questions in ADRE are deceptively common: new-district counts, largest and smallest, division membership, and which districts touch the international borders all appear regularly. This guide gives you the complete structure with every superlative the exam uses.

## Quick Answer: Districts of Assam Fact Sheet

| Fact | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Number of districts | 35 |
| Number of divisions | 5 (Upper, Middle, Lower Assam, BTR, Barak Valley) |
| Largest district | Karbi Anglong (~10,434 sq km) |
| Second largest | Dima Hasao (~4,888 sq km) |
| Smallest district | South Salmara-Mankachar (by area, among the newest) |
| Capital district | Kamrup Metropolitan (Dispur and Guwahati) |
| Only hill districts | Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao |
| Only hill station | Haflong (in Dima Hasao) [1] [2] |

## The Five Divisions and Their Districts

The divisional structure organises the state geographically and administratively [1] [2]:

| Division | Districts |
| --- | --- |
| Upper Assam | Tinsukia, Dibrugarh, Sivasagar, Charaideo, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Majuli |
| Middle Assam | Sonitpur, Biswanath, Golaghat, Nagaon, Hojai, Karbi Anglong, Dima Hasao |
| Lower Assam | Kamrup Metropolitan, Kamrup, Nalbari, Barpeta, Baksa, Udalguri, Darrang, Morigaon, Goalpara, South Salmara-Mankachar, Dhubri |
| Bodoland Territorial Region | Kokrajhar, Chirang, Baksa, Udalguri (BTR districts) |
| Barak Valley | Cachar, Karimganj, Hailakandi |

Note that **Baksa and Udalguri** belong simultaneously to Lower Assam division and to the **Bodoland Territorial Region** — the semi-autonomous council area created under the Sixth Schedule accords [1] [2].

## Superlatives and New Districts

The superlative facts are the most-marked. **Karbi Anglong** is the **largest district** at roughly 10,434 sq km, followed by **Dima Hasao** (~4,888 sq km) — together the state's only hill districts, sitting between the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys; **Haflong** in Dima Hasao is Assam's **only hill station** [1] [2] [3]. **South Salmara-Mankachar**, carved out of Dhubri in 2015, is the smallest by area, while **Majuli**, created in 2016, is India's **first island district** [2]. The state's newest wave came in **2022**, when six districts were created: **Bajali** (from Barpeta), **Biswanath and Hojai** (from Sonitpur and Nagaon), **South Salmara-Mankachar's** reorganisation, **Tamulpur** (from Chirang), and **West Karbi Anglong** (from Karbi Anglong) — taking the count from 31 (or 33 depending on the year of counting) to the present 35 [1] [2].

## Geography Notes per District

A few districts carry signature exam facts. **Dhubri** hosts the westernmost point and the Naranarayan Setu bridgehead; **Majuli** holds the world's largest river island and twenty-plus satras; **Dima Hasao** is the sole district south of the Barak valley; **Cachar's** Silchar is the Barak Valley's commercial hub; **Kamrup Metropolitan** contains the capital **Dispur**; and the border districts with Bangladesh — **Dhubri, Goalpara, Barpeta, Bongaigaon (now divided into Chirang/Baksa), Kokrajhar, Karimganj, Hailakandi, and Cachar** — anchor the state's southwestern frontier [1] [2].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many districts are there in Assam?

Thirty-five districts, organised into five administrative divisions [1] [2].

### What are the five divisions of Assam?

Upper Assam, Middle Assam, Lower Assam, Bodoland Territorial Region, and Barak Valley [1] [2].

### Which is the largest district of Assam?

Karbi Anglong, with nearly 10,434 sq km; Dima Hasao is second at about 4,888 sq km [1] [3].

### Which are the hill districts of Assam?

Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, the plateau regions between the Brahmaputra and Barak valleys; Haflong in Dima Hasao is the state's only hill station [2] [3].

### Which is Assam's island district?

Majuli, created in 2016 — India's first island district — formed from the world's largest river island in the Brahmaputra [2].

### How many new districts were created in Assam in 2022?

Six — including Bajali, Biswanath, Hojai, Tamulpur, West Karbi Anglong, and the reorganisation of South Salmara-Mankachar — raising the total to 35 [1] [2].
