## Majuli: The Largest River Island in the World

**Majuli** sits in the Brahmaputra opposite Jorhat, formed where the river splits around the island mass between the main channel and the Kherkutia Xuti branch [1] [2]. Recognised by the Guinness World Records as the **world's largest river island**, it covered roughly 1,250 sq km at the start of the 20th century; severe erosion has reduced it to about **880 sq km** — and the fight against the river's advance defines the island's modern story [1]. In **2016**, Majuli became **India's first island district**, carved out of Jorhat — a first no other state holds [1] [2].

## Satras and the Sattriya Tradition

Majuli is the spiritual capital of Assam's neo-Vaishnavite tradition. **Srimanta Sankardeva** and his disciple **Madhavdeva** established satras here from the 15th–16th centuries onward, and the island today hosts more than twenty functioning monasteries — **Auniati, Dakhinpat, Garamur, Kamalabari, and Bengenaati** among the famous — which preserve the manuscripts, mask-making (Mukha Shilpa), and the classical dance form **[Sattriya](#)**, one of India's eight classical dances [1] [2]. The island's communities — **Mising, Deuri, and Sonowal Kachari** — live alongside the satra culture, and Majuli's Raas Leela festival draws devotees across the winter [1].

## Umananda: The Peacock Island

Mid-river at Guwahati, **Umananda** — the "Peacock Island" — is one of the **world's smallest inhabited river islands** at roughly 0.012 sq km [1]. Its **Umananda Shiva temple**, associated with the legend of Shiva reducing Kamadeva to ashes, is reached by a short ferry from Kachari Ghat and is among the most-visited religious sites of the state [1] [2]. The contrast between the two islands — Majuli's vast cultural landscape and Umananda's tiny devotional rock — is a favourite ADRE comparison.

## Other River Islands

The Brahmaputra continuously births and buries smaller islands — **chars** — across its length: Majuli's satellite bars, the islands near **Dibrugarh and Dhemaji**, and the shifting sand-islands of the lower valley [1] [2]. Unlike Majuli and Umananda, these chars are temporary by nature; their impermanence is itself a lesson in the Brahmaputra's restless geomorphology, and in the vulnerability of Assam's riverside populations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the largest river island in the world?

Majuli, in the Brahmaputra river in Jorhat district, Assam — recognised by Guinness World Records [1] [2].

### When did Majuli become a district?

In 2016, becoming India's first island district [1].

### Why has Majuli's area decreased?

Severe river erosion by the Brahmaputra has reduced the island from about 1,250 sq km a century ago to roughly 880 sq km today [1].

### What is Umananda Island?

The "Peacock Island" at Guwahati — among the world's smallest inhabited river islands — home to the ancient Umananda Shiva temple [1] [2].

### What is Majuli famous for culturally?

Its 22+ Vaishnavite satras, the Sattriya classical dance tradition, mask-making arts, and the Mising, Deuri, and Sonowal Kachari communities [1] [2].

### What are chars in Assam?

Temporary sand and silt river islands continuously formed and eroded by the Brahmaputra along its course [1] [2].
