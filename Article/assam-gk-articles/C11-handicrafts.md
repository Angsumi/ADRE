## Sarthebari: The Brass Village

**Sarthebari** in Barpeta district is Assam's celebrated centre of **bell-metal and brass** craft, producing the ceremonial **xorai** (the bell-metal tray with lid used to offer respect), **kalah** (water pots), and **bota** sets — the ritual metalware present in every Assamese household's *xorai-bota* etiquette [1] [2]. The craft earned a **GI tag**, and Sarthebari's artisans supply naamghars, satras, and homes across the state; the xorai remains the most universally recognised object of Assamese ceremonial culture [2].

## Sualkuchi and the Three Silks

**Sualkuchi**, on the north bank of the Brahmaputra in Kamrup district, is the weaving capital of Assam — popularly called the **"Manchester of Assam"** — where Muga, Pat, and Eri silks are woven into mekhela-chadors, rihas, and shawls on traditional looms [1] [2]. The three silks carry distinct identities: **Muga**, the golden silk produced only in Assam (GI-protected, the state's signature luxury fabric); **Pat**, the gleaming white mulberry silk; and **Eri**, the "peace silk" (ahimsa) spun without killing the pupa, warm and worn in winter [1] [2].

## Majuli Masks and the Bamboo World

**Majuli's mask-making (mukha shilpa)** — the art of creating the giant, expressive masks used in Bhaona performances — is a **GI-tagged** satra craft passed through generations of artisan-monks [1] [2]. Alongside it, Assam's **cane and bamboo** craft is the state's largest rural industry: mats, baskets, fishing traps (jakoi), fans, furniture, and the architectural bamboo of rural homes — with **Jonai** (Dhemaji) and BTR villages among the great production centres [1]. The gamosa and riya weaving of Kamrup and Nalbari round out a craft economy that turns village labour into GI-protected heritage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which place is famous for bell metal craft in Assam?

Sarthebari in Barpeta district — the "brass village of Assam," whose xorai-bota metalware carries a GI tag [1] [2].

### What is Sualkuchi known as?

The "Manchester of Assam" — the state's silk weaving capital, producing Muga, Pat, and Eri silks [1] [2].

### What is Mukha Shilpa?

The GI-tagged mask-making art of Majuli, creating the masks used in Bhaona theatre performances [1] [2].

### Which silk is exclusive to Assam?

Muga — the golden silk, GI-protected and produced nowhere else in the world [1] [2].

### What is Eri silk?

"Peace silk" (ahimsa silk) spun without killing the silkworm pupa — a warm winter fabric of Assam [1] [2].

### What is the xorai?

The bell-metal tray with a domed lid used in Assamese households to offer respect to guests and during rituals [1] [2].
