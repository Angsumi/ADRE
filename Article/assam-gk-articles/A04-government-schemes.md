## Orunodoi: The Flagship Direct Benefit Programme

**Orunodoi** began in 2020 as a Rs 830-to-1,250-per-month assistance programme to around 19 lakh financially vulnerable households, paid directly to women beneficiaries through bank accounts [1] [2]. In **September 2024**, **Orunodoi 3.0** relaunched the programme targeting over **37 lakh beneficiaries**, absorbing four existing schemes — the **Deen Dayal Divyangjan Achoni** (divyangjan support), **Indira Miri** (widow pension), **Sarbojanin** (old-age pension), and **Biswa Pension Achoni** — into a single direct-benefit platform, making it **Assam's largest DBT scheme** [1] [2]. Its design — cash to women, Aadhaar-linked bank transfer — is a model the exam's polity questions frequently cite.

## Arundhati, Atal Amrit, and the Rest

The **Arundhati Gold Scheme** (2019) gifts **30 grams of gold** to every bride from an SC household at marriage; the enhanced **Arundhati 2.0** (2021) replaced the physical gift with a **Rs 5 lakh direct cash benefit** [1] [2]. **Atal Amrit Abhiyan** (2022) extended free health insurance of **Rs 2.5 lakh per family per year** to all residents — including those outside the national Ayushman cards — through empanelled hospitals [2]. **Amrit Brikshya Andolan** (2022) committed to planting **one crore saplings**, among India's largest afforestation drives [2]. **Mission Basudha** digitised land pattas; the **Chah Bagicha Dhan Puraskar** pays tea-garden workers directly; and **Aponjon** (2013) pioneered maternal-newborn incentives that later informed national schemes [1] [2].

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Orunodoi 3.0?

Assam's flagship direct-benefit scheme relaunched in September 2024 for vulnerable women-headed households, integrating four pension and support schemes into the state's largest DBT programme [1] [2].

### What does the Arundhati Gold Scheme provide?

30 grams of gold for SC-bride marriages (2019); the enhanced Arundhati 2.0 (2021) gives a direct benefit of Rs 5 lakh instead [1] [2].

### What is Atal Amrit Abhiyan?

Assam's 2022 health-insurance scheme providing Rs 2.5 lakh annual family cover to all residents, including those without national health cards [2].

### What is Amrit Brikshya Andolan?

The 2022 afforestation drive targeting the planting of one crore saplings across Assam [2].

### What is Mission Basudha?

The 2021 programme digitising land records and pattas across all 35 districts of Assam [1] [2].

### Which schemes were merged into Orunodoi 3.0?

Deen Dayal Divyangjan Achoni, Indira Miri, Sarbojanin, and Biswa Pension Achoni [1] [2].
