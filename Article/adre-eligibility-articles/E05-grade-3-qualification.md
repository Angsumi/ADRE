**Grade 3** is the graduate tier of the Assam Direct Recruitment, and its **qualification requirements** vary by post: while the baseline is a **bachelor's degree**, the post annexures layer on computer certificates, stream filters, and professional qualifying tests [1] [2]. This guide walks through the major post families so you can match your degree to the right post codes.

## Quick Answer: Grade 3 Post Qualifications

| Post family | Base qualification | Additional requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior Assistant (JA) | Bachelor's degree (any stream) | Computer literacy; typing qualifying test in some departments |
| Accounts Junior (AJ) | Bachelor's degree (Commerce preferred) | Accounts/computer skills; stream-filtered annexures |
| Lower Division Assistant (LDA/LDC) | Bachelor's degree | Typing skill (35 wpm English / 30 wpm Assamese) as qualifying test |
| Technical assistants | Bachelor's in relevant stream (Science/Engineering) | Stream-specific annexure |
| Library / specialist posts | Degree + library science or professional qualification | Recognised professional certificate |

## The Baseline: Bachelor's Degree

Every Grade 3 post demands a **degree from a recognised university** — Assam's universities or any UGC-recognised institution in India, including recognised open-university degrees [1] [2]. The age band is **18–40**, the pay scale sits at **Pay Levels 5–8** (entry basics of roughly Rs 22,700–35,400 plus allowances), and the exam is the standard **five-paper objective structure** with 0.25 negative marking [1] [2].

## The Computer-Certificate Wave

The most significant qualification shift in recent rounds: **many clerical Grade 3 posts now require a computer certificate or diploma of at least six months** in addition to the degree [1] [2]. Candidates without one can still apply to non-filtered posts, but the filtered posts — an increasing share of the clerical block — reject applications missing the certificate. If you are within a year of the next notification, completing a recognised six-month computer course is the highest-value eligibility investment available to a Grade 3 aspirant [2].

## Professional Qualifying Tests

Certain posts add a **skill gate after the written exam**: typing tests (typically **35 words per minute in English or 30 wpm in Assamese**) for LDC-type posts, or computer-proficiency tests for data-oriented posts [1] [2]. These are qualifying, not ranking — pass/fail — but failure eliminates you regardless of written scores, so Grade 3 candidates targeting clerical posts should practise typing from day one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the qualification for Junior Assistant in ADRE?

A bachelor's degree (any stream) from a recognised university, with computer literacy; some departments add a typing qualifying test [1] [2].

### Do all Grade 3 posts need a computer certificate?

No — only posts whose annexure specifies it; the requirement has been expanding across clerical posts since ADRE 3.0 [1] [2].

### Is commerce mandatory for Accounts Junior?

The annexure specifies the stream per post family; commerce degrees are required or strongly preferred for most accounts posts [1] [2].

### What is the typing speed requirement?

Typically 35 words per minute in English or 30 wpm in Assamese as a qualifying (pass/fail) test [1] [2].

### What is the age limit for Grade 3?

18–40 years as of the notification reference date, with category relaxations per the [age relaxation](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/age-relaxation/) rules [1] [2].

### What pay level do Grade 3 posts offer?

Pay Levels 5–8, with entry basics around Rs 22,700–35,400 plus DA, HRA, and transport [1] [2].
