**PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities)** candidates enjoy ADRE's most substantial concessions — **age relaxation of up to 10 years for general PwBD and up to 15 years for SC/ST PwBD**, plus **4% reservation** under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 — subject to the post being identified as suitable for the disability class [1] [2]. This guide explains the certification chain, the concessions, and how post identification works.

## Quick Answer: PwBD Benefits in ADRE

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Definition | Benchmark disability: 40%+ certified disability under RPwD Act 2016 |
| Age relaxation (general PwBD) | Up to +10 years (Grade 3 ceiling ~50; Grade 4 ~48) |
| Age relaxation (SC/ST PwBD) | Up to +15 years (Grade 3 ceiling ~55; Grade 4 ~53) |
| Reservation | 4% of vacancies in identified posts (RPwD Act 2016) |
| Certificate | Government medical board certificate in prescribed format |
| Post condition | Post must be identified as suitable for the disability class |

## Benchmark Disability and Certification

The gateway document is the **benchmark disability certificate** — certification of **40% or greater disability** issued by a **government medical board** in the format prescribed under the RPwD Act, 2016 [1] [2]. Private hospital assessments, older pre-2016 formats, or certificates specifying disabilities below the benchmark threshold do not qualify. The certificate must specify the **disability class** (visual, hearing, locomotor, intellectual, mental illness, or specified categories under the Act), because post suitability is matched class by class.

## The Concessions

The age relaxations follow central (DoPT) norms adopted by Assam: **up to ten years** for general-category PwBD — lifting Grade 3's ceiling to roughly **50** — and **up to fifteen years** for SC/ST PwBD, with ceilings approaching **55** [1] [2]. These are the largest relaxations in any Assam recruitment, reflecting the statutory force of the RPwD Act. The **4% reservation** applies across identified posts, with horizontal application across the SC/ST/OBC quotas where applicable [2].

## Post Identification: The Condition That Matters Most

PwBD reservation attaches only to **posts identified in the notification annexure as suitable** for particular disability classes — a typing-intensive clerical post may be identified for visual impairment but not for certain locomotor classes, and vice versa [1] [2]. Before applying, cross-reference your disability class against the annexure's identified-post table; claiming PwBD status for a non-identified post forfeits the reservation and, in some cases, the relaxation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What counts as a benchmark disability in ADRE?

A disability of 40% or more certified by a government medical board under the RPwD Act, 2016 [1] [2].

### How much age relaxation do PwBD candidates get?

Up to 10 years for general PwBD (Grade 3 ceiling ~50) and up to 15 years for SC/ST PwBD (ceiling ~55) [1] [2].

### What reservation do PwBD candidates get?

4% of vacancies in posts identified as suitable for their disability class, per the RPwD Act 2016 [1] [2].

### Can I claim PwBD relaxation for any post?

Only for posts identified in the notification annexure as suitable for your disability class [1] [2].

### Are private medical certificates accepted?

No — only government medical board certificates in the prescribed RPwD format qualify [1] [2].

### Does PwBD relaxation stack with SC/ST relaxation?

SC/ST PwBD candidates receive the higher (up to 15-year) relaxation; relaxations are not additively stacked [1] [2].
