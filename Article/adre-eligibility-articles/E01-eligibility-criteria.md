Before you spend hours filling the application, the first question every ADRE candidate must answer is: **am I actually eligible?** The **ADRE eligibility criteria** rest on five pillars — **nationality, age, education, domicile, and category certification** — all of which have remained stable across ADRE 2.0 and 3.0 [1] [2]. Getting any one of them wrong leads to rejection at document verification, no matter how well you write the exam. This guide gives you the complete checklist.

## Quick Answer: Eligibility Criteria at a Glance

| Criterion | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Nationality | Indian citizen |
| Age (Grade 3) | 18–40 years as of the notification cut-off date |
| Age (Grade 4) | 18–38 years as of the notification cut-off date |
| Education (Grade 3) | Bachelor's degree; some posts require a computer certificate/diploma or specific streams |
| Education (Grade 4) | HSLC/Matriculation, HS pass, or honours — post-dependent |
| Domicile | Born in Assam, or land records, or 10-year residency proof |
| Age proof | HSLC/Matriculation certificate only — Aadhaar and birth certificates are not accepted |
| Category | SC/ST/OBC/MOBC/PwBD/ESM certifications where claimed, as per Assam government norms |

## Nationality and Domicile: The Two Gates

ADRE is **Assam's own recruitment**: candidates must be **Indian citizens** and must establish a **domicile connection to Assam** through one of three accepted routes — being **born in Assam** (birth certificate), holding **ancestral land records** in the state, or proving **ten years of continuous residency** with supporting documentation [1] [2]. These routes have been consistent since ADRE 1.0, though 3.0 tightened verification through Aadhaar-linked checks [2].

## Age and Education: The Core Filters

The age architecture is simple: **18–40 for Grade 3** and **18–38 for Grade 4**, calculated against the cut-off date stated in each notification (typically 1 January of the notification year), with relaxations for reserved categories covered in the [age relaxation](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/age-relaxation/) article [1] [2]. Education follows the grade logic: **every Grade 3 post demands a bachelor's degree**, with certain posts adding a computer certificate or diploma (minimum six months) or specifying streams; **Grade 4 posts range from HSLC (Junior Grade Assistant) to HS pass to honours (Driver)** [1] [2].

## The Age-Proof Trap

One rule catches thousands of candidates every round: **ADRE accepts only the HSLC/Matriculation certificate as proof of age** — Aadhaar cards, voter IDs, and birth certificates are explicitly not accepted for this purpose [1] [2]. If your matriculation certificate contains a different name or date of birth from your degree certificates, resolve the discrepancy (affidavit, gazette notification) *before* applying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is eligible to apply for ADRE?

Indian citizens aged 18–40 (Grade 3) or 18–38 (Grade 4) with the required education and Assam domicile connection [1] [2].

### Is ADRE open to candidates from other states?

Only with documented Assam connection: birth in Assam, ancestral land records, or ten years of residency [1] [2].

### What is the age cut-off date?

The notification specifies the reference date (typically 1 January of the notification year); your age must fall within limits on that date [1] [2].

### Can final-year students apply?

Generally no — the bachelor's degree must be completed by the notification cut-off date; check the specific notification annexure [1] [2].

### What if my HSLC age proof differs from other documents?

Obtain an affidavit and gazette notification to regularise the discrepancy before applying — age is verified solely through the matriculation certificate [1] [2].

### Is there an attempt limit?

No — ADRE has no attempt limit; you may apply in every round while within the age ceiling [1] [2].
