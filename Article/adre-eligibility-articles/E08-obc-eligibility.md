**OBC eligibility in ADRE** hinges on Assam's distinctive two-track system: the general **OBC** category and the state-specific **MOBC** (Muslim Other Backward Classes) category — both carrying a **three-year age relaxation** and reservation quotas [1] [2]. Getting the category right matters enormously: an MOBC candidate who applies under general OBC (or vice versa) risks rejection at verification. This guide separates the two clearly.

## Quick Answer: OBC and MOBC Benefits in ADRE

| Benefit | OBC | MOBC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Age relaxation | +3 years (Grade 3 ceiling: 43; Grade 4: 41) | +3 years (same ceilings) |
| Certificate | OBC certificate from competent Assam authority | MOBC certificate (Assam-specific format) |
| Quota | OBC reservation quota per Assam norms | MOBC reservation quota |
| Income criterion | As per Assam government norms | As per Assam government norms |
| Age proof | HSLC certificate (all candidates) | HSLC certificate |

## OBC: The Standard Category

Assam's OBC category follows the national framework under **Article 16(4)** with state-specific notified lists: candidates must appear on the **Assam government's notified OBC list** and hold a certificate from the competent state authority [1] [2]. The relaxation adds **three years** to both grades — Grade 3's ceiling rises from 40 to **43**, Grade 4's from 38 to **41** [1] [2].

## MOBC: Assam's Signature Category

The **MOBC** category — recognition of backward classes among Assam's Muslim population — is unique to Assam's reservation architecture, with its own notified list, certificate format, and quota [2]. MOBC certificates are issued by designated Assam authorities in a distinct format; a generic OBC certificate does not substitute, and the relaxation and quota attach only to the correctly certified category [1] [2].

## The Certification Traps

Three failure modes dominate OBC/MOBC verification. First, **out-of-state certificates** — OBC certificates from other states are not accepted for Assam's quota [1] [2]. Second, **category mismatch** — applying as OBC when the candidate's community is notified under MOBC (or the reverse) voids the claim. Third, **income-criterion shifts** — Assam periodically revises the creamy-layer-equivalent thresholds; always check the current notification's income annexure rather than relying on older rounds [2].

## Claiming Your Category Correctly

Before applying, confirm your community appears on the **current notified list** under the category you intend to claim, obtain the matching certificate from the competent authority, and cross-check the income criterion. The [eligibility checker](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/eligibility-checker/) computes your relaxed ceiling once the category is confirmed; the [documents required](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/documents-required/) article lists the certificate formats, and the [age relaxation](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/age-relaxation/) article places OBC within the full concession framework. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What age relaxation do OBC candidates get in ADRE?

Three years — Grade 3 ceiling becomes 43 and Grade 4 becomes 41 [1] [2].

### What is MOBC and how is it different from OBC?

MOBC (Muslim Other Backward Classes) is an Assam-specific category with its own notified list, certificate, and quota — distinct from the general OBC category [1] [2].

### Can I use another state's OBC certificate?

No — ADRE requires OBC/MOBC certificates issued by competent Assam authorities [1] [2].

### Is there an income limit for OBC in ADRE?

Assam applies income criteria per its norms, revised periodically — check the current notification's annexure [2].

### What happens if I claim the wrong category?

The reservation and relaxation are voided and the application risks rejection at document verification [1] [2].

### How is the MOBC certificate obtained?

From designated Assam authorities in the MOBC-specific format; processing takes weeks, so apply early [1] [2].
