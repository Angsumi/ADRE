The **ADRE educational qualification** rules follow a clean grade logic — **a bachelor's degree for every Grade 3 post and school-level qualifications for Grade 4** — but post-specific annexures add computer certificates, stream filters, and licence conditions that trip up otherwise well-prepared candidates [1] [2]. This guide covers the framework and its exceptions.

## Quick Answer: Educational Qualification Summary

| Post level | Base qualification | Common additions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grade 3 — clerical (JA, AA, LDC) | Bachelor's degree (any stream) | Computer certificate/diploma (min. 6 months) for many posts |
| Grade 3 — accounts/technical | Bachelor's in Commerce/Science | Specific stream + computer skills |
| Grade 3 — specialist | Degree in the relevant field | Professional certification as annexured |
| Grade 4 — Junior Grade Assistant | HSLC/Matriculation | — |
| Grade 4 — HS-level posts | HS (10+2) pass | — |
| Grade 4 — Driver | Honours pass | Valid driving licence (LMV) |

## The Degree Requirement for Grade 3

Every Grade 3 post requires a **bachelor's degree from a recognised university**, and ADRE accepts degrees from Assam's universities (Gauhati, Dibrugarh, Cotton, Tezpur, Assam University, and others) as well as degrees from recognised universities anywhere in India [1] [2]. Two refinements matter. First, **many clerical posts now require a computer certificate or diploma of at least six months** — a condition that grew prominent in ADRE 3.0 and reflects the digitised nature of clerical work [1] [2]. Second, accounts and technical posts filter by **stream**: a Bachelor of Commerce for accounts juniors, science degrees for technical assistants, and so on — always check the post annexure.

## School-Level Qualifications for Grade 4

Grade 4 splits into three educational tiers: **HSLC/Matriculation** for Junior Grade Assistant and most service posts, **HS (10+2) pass** for higher support posts, and **honours plus a valid driving licence** for Driver posts [1] [2]. As with age, the **HSLC certificate doubles as the age-proof document**, so its integrity — name spelling, date of birth, board recognition — matters doubly for Grade 4 applicants.

## What Counts as "Recognised"

ADRE accepts qualifications from **universities and boards recognised by UGC/AICTE and the respective statutory bodies**, including open and distance learning degrees from recognised institutions (IGNOU, state open universities) [1] [2]. Private or unrecognised institutes' diplomas do not qualify. Candidates awaiting final results at application time should check the notification's cut-off clause — most ADRE rounds require the qualification to be **completed by the notification's reference date**, not by the joining date.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum qualification for ADRE Grade 3?

A bachelor's degree from a recognised university; many posts additionally require a computer certificate or diploma of at least six months [1] [2].

### Are distance-education degrees accepted?

Yes, from UGC-recognised open universities such as IGNOU and state open universities [1] [2].

### Is a percentage requirement specified?

ADRE sets **pass** as the standard — no minimum percentage is generally required unless a post annexure specifies otherwise [1] [2].

### Can final-year students apply?

Usually no — the degree must be completed by the notification's reference date; confirm in the annexure [1] [2].

### What does a Grade 4 Driver post need?

An honours pass plus a valid light-motor-vehicle driving licence [1] [2].

### Is the HSLC certificate used for anything else?

Yes — it is the sole accepted proof of age in ADRE, in addition to proving Grade 4 educational eligibility [1] [2].
