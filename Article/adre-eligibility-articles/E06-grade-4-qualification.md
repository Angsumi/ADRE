**Grade 4** is the school-level tier of the Assam Direct Recruitment — the most accessible government entry point in the state — with qualifications ranging from **HSLC/Matriculation to honours plus a driving licence** depending on the post [1] [2]. This guide maps each post family to its exact requirement so you apply to the right post codes the first time.

## Quick Answer: Grade 4 Post Qualifications

| Post family | Qualification | Additional requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior Grade Assistant (JGA) | HSLC / Matriculation | — |
| HS-level support posts | HS (10+2) pass | — |
| Driver | Honours pass | Valid LMV driving licence; medical fitness test |
| Class IV / service posts | HSLC or as annexured | Medical fitness for specified posts |

## The Baseline: HSLC Opens the Door

The **HSLC/Matriculation certificate** is ADRE Grade 4's master key: it proves both your **educational eligibility** for JGA and service posts and your **age** (it is the sole accepted age-proof document in ADRE) [1] [2]. The age band is **18–38** (some notifications carry Grade 4 posts to 40 — check the annexure), pay sits at **Pay Levels 2–4** (entry basics roughly Rs 14,000–25,500 plus allowances), and the exam follows the same **five-paper objective structure** as Grade 3, calibrated to HS depth [1] [2].

## The Driver Post Exception

The **Driver** post is Grade 4's qualification outlier: it requires an **honours pass plus a valid light-motor-vehicle driving licence**, and selected candidates must clear a **medical fitness test** (vision, general health) before joining [1] [2]. This combination — higher education plus a licence plus medical clearance — makes Driver posts simultaneously the most demanding and among the best-paid Grade 4 roles, given transport allowances.

## Medical Fitness

Beyond the Driver post, several Grade 4 service posts require a **medical fitness certificate** at the verification or joining stage [1] [2]. Candidates with correctable vision or manageable conditions should anticipate a medical board and carry relevant records; fitness standards for physical-service posts are specified per post annexure.

## Why Grade 4 Is the Smart First Step

Grade 4's school-level bar, combined with ADRE's 38-year age ceiling, makes it the highest-probability government entry for school-pass candidates — and once inside, **departmental promotion exams** open the path to Grade 3 ranks [1] [2]. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the qualification for Junior Grade Assistant?

HSLC/Matriculation — the minimum educational bar for most Grade 4 posts [1] [2].

### What does a Driver post require?

An honours pass, a valid LMV driving licence, and a medical fitness test [1] [2].

### Is the HSLC certificate used for age proof?

Yes — ADRE accepts only the matriculation certificate as proof of age, making it doubly important for Grade 4 applicants [1] [2].

### What is the age limit for Grade 4?

18–38 years as of the notification reference date (some posts to 40), with category relaxations [1] [2].

### What pay level do Grade 4 posts offer?

Pay Levels 2–4, entry basics around Rs 14,000–25,500 plus DA and allowances [1] [2].

### Can Grade 4 employees get promoted?

Yes — departmental promotion examinations allow movement into Grade 3 cadres after joining [1] [2].
