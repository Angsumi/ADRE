Manually computing ADRE eligibility is error-prone: age against a notification reference date, category relaxations, post-specific computer certificates, licence conditions — one miscalculation can cost a full recruitment cycle. Our free **ADRE eligibility calculator** does all of it in seconds: enter your profile and it tells you exactly which **grades and posts you qualify for**, and how your category relaxations extend your window [1] [2].

## Quick Answer: What the Calculator Checks

| Input | What it computes |
| --- | --- |
| Date of birth | Exact eligibility vs the Grade 3 (40) and Grade 4 (38) ceilings |
| Category (UR/SC/ST/OBC/MOBC/PwBD/ESM) | Your relaxed upper age limit per Assam norms |
| Education | Qualification match against Grade 3 and Grade 4 post requirements |
| Domicile status | Assam connection (birth / land records / 10-year residency) |
| Certificates held | Computer course, driving licence, medical fitness flags |

## How to Use It

Open the **[eligibility checker](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/eligibility-checker/)** and fill in five fields: your **date of birth**, **highest qualification**, **category**, **domicile route**, and any **additional certificates** (computer course, licence). The calculator returns a clear result: which grade(s) you are eligible for, your effective age ceiling after relaxations, and any post-level conditions you should be aware of — for example, whether your profile meets the computer-certificate requirement for filtered clerical posts [1] [2].

## Why a Calculator Beats Manual Checking

Three reasons. First, **reference-date arithmetic is unintuitive**: whether you qualify depends on your age on the notification's fixed date (typically 1 January of the notification year), not on today's date — the calculator uses the correct reference automatically [1] [2]. Second, **relaxation stacking is confusing**: SC/ST PwBD candidates, ex-servicemen with long service, and MOBC applicants each have different ceiling rules that are easy to misapply by hand [1] [2]. Third, **post-level conditions are hidden in annexures**: the calculator flags the computer-certificate and licence filters that annexures bury, before you waste an application fee.

## What to Do With the Result

If the calculator confirms eligibility, move immediately to the application-preparation stage: order your **domicile and category certificates** (the full paperwork sequence is in the [documents required](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/documents-required/) article), confirm the post list on the [Grade 3](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/grade-3/) and [Grade 4](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/grade-4/) pages, and start the [30-day study plan](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/30-day-study-plan/). If the result shows a gap — say, a missing computer certificate — the timeline is explicit: a recognised six-month course completed before the next notification restores eligibility. The calculator also pairs with the broader criteria guides: [eligibility criteria](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/eligibility-criteria/), [age limit](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/age-limit/), [age relaxation](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/age-relaxation/), and [educational qualification](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre-eligibility/educational-qualification/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the ADRE eligibility calculator free?

Yes — the [eligibility checker](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/eligibility-checker/) is completely free with no registration required.

### What inputs does it need?

Date of birth, highest qualification, category, domicile route, and optional certificates (computer course, driving licence) [1] [2].

### Does it apply age relaxations automatically?

Yes — it computes your relaxed ceiling for SC/ST (+5), OBC/MOBC (+3), PwBD (up to +10/+15), and ex-servicemen (service + 3) per Assam norms [1] [2].

### How accurate is it?

It implements the official criteria from recent ADRE notifications; always confirm against the final notification annexure, which governs each round [1] [2].

### Can it check specific posts?

It confirms grade-level eligibility and flags post-level conditions; final post matching uses the [Grade 3](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/grade-3/) and [Grade 4](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/grade-4/) directories [1] [2].

### What if I am not currently eligible?

The result explains the gap (age, education, certificate, or domicile) and what to fix before the next round — for most candidates that is a six-month computer course [1] [2].
