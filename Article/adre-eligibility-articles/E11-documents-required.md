ADRE applications live and die on paperwork. Candidates clear the exam and then fail at **document verification** because a certificate was missing, misspelled, or in the wrong format [1] [2]. This guide gives the **complete documents checklist** for every stage — application, admit card, exam day, and verification — so nothing surprises you.

## Quick Answer: Documents by Stage

| Stage | Documents |
| --- | --- |
| Application (online) | Photo, signature, HSLC certificate data, degree details, Aadhaar (3.0 seeding), category certificates (if claiming) |
| Exam day (admit card) | Admit card + one photo ID (Aadhaar recommended) |
| Document verification / joining | Full originals set — see detailed checklist below |

## The Core Set: What Every Candidate Needs

The universal documents are the **HSLC/Matriculation certificate** (the sole accepted age proof — Aadhaar, voter ID, and birth certificates are explicitly not accepted for age), **degree certificates and mark sheets** for Grade 3 applicants, and **Aadhaar** — which ADRE 3.0 made mandatory for seeding with the application [1] [2]. Every name and date of birth must match across these documents; discrepancies require an affidavit and gazette notification *before* verification.

## Domicile Proof: The Three Routes

ADRE accepts domicile through **birth in Assam** (birth certificate, school records showing Assam schooling), **ancestral land records** in the state (patta, land deed, mutation records), or **ten years of continuous residency** with supporting documentation (residence certificates, utility records, employment records) [1] [2]. Start the domicile certificate process at your circle office early — issuance commonly takes several weeks.

## Category-Specific Documents

Reserved-category claims demand their matching certificates: **SC/ST certificates** from competent Assam authorities (note the tea-garden community spellings for SC), **OBC or MOBC certificates** in the Assam-specific formats, **PwBD certificates** from a government medical board under the RPwD Act format, and **ESM documents** — discharge book, release orders, and pension papers [1] [2]. Computer-literacy claims (for filtered Grade 3 posts) need the **six-month-or-longer computer course certificate**, and Driver posts need the **valid LMV licence** plus medical fitness papers.

## The Name-Match Rule

The single most-quoted verification failure in Assam recruitments: **name spellings that differ across documents**. If your HSLC certificate reads one spelling, your degree another, and your Aadhaar a third, obtain an affidavit, publish a gazette notification, and carry both original and corrected documents to verification [1] [2].

## Your Paperwork Timeline

Work backwards from the notification: order domicile and category certificates **8–10 weeks before** applications open, resolve name discrepancies **before** the portal opens, and scan every document in the portal's specified format and size. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the age proof document in ADRE?

Only the HSLC/Matriculation certificate — Aadhaar, voter ID, and birth certificates are not accepted for age proof [1] [2].

### What domicile documents are accepted?

Three routes: birth in Assam, ancestral land records, or ten years of residency with supporting proof [1] [2].

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for ADRE?

From ADRE 3.0 onward, Aadhaar seeding is mandatory with the online application [1] [2].

### What if my name differs across documents?

Obtain an affidavit and gazette notification to regularise before document verification [1] [2].

### Which computer certificate is accepted for Grade 3 posts?

A recognised computer course certificate or diploma of at least six months, where the post annexure requires it [1] [2].

### When should I start collecting documents?

Eight to ten weeks before the notification — domicile and category certificates take the longest to issue [1] [2].
