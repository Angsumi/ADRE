The **ADRE age relaxation** rules extend the standard 18–40 (Grade 3) and 18–38 (Grade 4) ceilings for reserved categories — and they are frequently the difference between eligibility and rejection for candidates at the margin [1] [2]. Assam's framework combines national norms (SC/ST, PwBD, ex-servicemen) with state-specific categories (OBC/MOBC), making the category-wise ceiling different for almost every applicant type. This guide gives you the exact concessions.

## Quick Answer: Category-wise Age Relaxation

| Category | Relaxation | Effective upper limit (Grade 3) | Effective upper limit (Grade 4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General / UR | None | 40 years | 38 years |
| SC / ST | +5 years | 45 years | 43 years |
| OBC / MOBC | +3 years | 43 years | 41 years |
| PwBD (general) | Up to +10 years | Up to 50 years | Up to 48 years |
| PwBD (SC/ST) | Up to +15 years | Up to 55 years | Up to 53 years |
| Ex-servicemen | Service period + 3 years | Per service length | Per service length |
| Disabled defence personnel | Up to 45–50 years | Per Assam norms | Per Assam norms |

## The Standard Concessions

Under Assam government recruitment norms applied in ADRE notifications, **SC and ST candidates receive a five-year relaxation** and **OBC/MOBC candidates receive three years** on the upper age limit [1] [2]. The **MOBC** category — Muslim Other Backward Classes — is an Assam-specific recognition worth noting, as is the state's distinct reservation treatment of the tea-garden communities within the SC quota [2]. These relaxations apply to both grades and are calculated against the same notification reference date as the general limit.

## PwBD: The Largest Concession

Candidates with **benchmark disabilities** (as defined under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016) receive the most substantial relief — **up to ten years for general-category PwBD and up to fifteen years for SC/ST PwBD**, following central (DoPT) norms adopted by Assam [1] [2]. Two conditions attach: the disability must be certified by a **government medical board**, and the post must be identified as suitable for that disability class in the notification annexure — not all posts qualify for PwBD reservation [2].

## Ex-Servicemen Rules

**Ex-servicemen** — retired or released defence personnel — receive a relaxation of their **period of military service plus three years** for Group B and C (non-gazetted) posts under Assam's norms, with **disabled defence service personnel eligible up to 45 or 50 years** depending on the post [1] [2]. The discharge book and service records form the proof, and the relaxation is computed on the actual length of service rather than a flat number.

## Claiming the Relaxation Correctly

Relaxations are never automatic: you must **hold the valid category certificate at application time** (SC/ST/OBC/MOBC certificates issued by competent Assam authorities, PwBD certificate from a government medical board, discharge documentation for ESM), tick the correct category box in the portal, and upload the certificate in the specified format [1] [2]. A wrongly claimed category invites rejection at document verification even if the exam was cleared. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much age relaxation do SC/ST candidates get in ADRE?

Five years — raising the Grade 3 ceiling to 45 and Grade 4 to 43 [1] [2].

### What is the OBC/MOBC relaxation?

Three years — Grade 3 ceiling becomes 43, Grade 4 becomes 41 [1] [2].

### Is MOBC the same as OBC?

No — MOBC (Muslim Other Backward Classes) is an Assam-specific category with its own certificate; both get the three-year relaxation [1] [2].

### How much relaxation do PwBD candidates get?

Up to 10 years for general PwBD (ceiling ~50 for Grade 3) and up to 15 years for SC/ST PwBD, subject to the post being identified as suitable [1] [2].

### How is ex-servicemen relaxation calculated?

Period of military service plus three years for non-gazetted posts, proved through discharge documents [1] [2].

### Do relaxations apply to the minimum age?

No — relaxations apply only to the upper age limit; the minimum age remains 18 for all categories [1] [2].
