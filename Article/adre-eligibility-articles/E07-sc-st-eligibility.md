**SC/ST candidates** form the backbone of ADRE's reservation framework, with benefits spanning **vacancy quotas, a five-year age relaxation, and category-specific fees** [1] [2]. Assam's SC category carries a distinctive feature — the inclusion of **tea-garden communities** — that candidates outside the state rarely encounter. This guide covers the complete SC/ST eligibility picture.

## Quick Answer: SC/ST Benefits in ADRE

| Benefit | SC | ST |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Age relaxation | +5 years (Grade 3 ceiling: 45; Grade 4: 43) | +5 years (same ceilings) |
| Vacancy reservation | Constitutional quota (Art. 16(4)); includes tea-garden communities | Quota for plains and hill tribes of Assam |
| Age proof | HSLC certificate (same as all candidates) | HSLC certificate |
| Certificate issuer | Competent Assam authority (district-level SC certificate) | Competent Assam authority (ST certificate) |
| Fee concessions | As per notification | As per notification |

## The Reservation Quotas

Under **Article 16(4) of the Constitution**, ADRE notifications reserve vacancies for SC and ST candidates in proportion to Assam's population composition, applied post-wise and roster-wise [1] [2]. Assam's SC quota is distinctive nationally because it extends to **tea-garden community candidates** who satisfy the state's SC certification norms — a historically significant provision given the community's role in Assam's economy [2]. The ST quota similarly covers both plains tribes and the state's hill communities, with certificates issued by Assam's competent district authorities.

## Age Relaxation in Practice

The **five-year relaxation** lifts the Grade 3 ceiling from 40 to **45** and the Grade 4 ceiling from 38 to **43** [1] [2]. For a 42-year-old SC graduate, this is the difference between ineligibility and a full application — the single most consequential benefit in the entire criteria set. The relaxation is computed against the notification's reference date exactly like the general limit.

## The Certificate Rules That Decide Everything

The reservation is only as good as the certificate: SC/ST status must be proved through **certificates issued by competent Assam authorities** — typically the circle officer or deputy commissioner level — in the format specified by the notification [1] [2]. Certificates from other states, expired formats, or incorrect community spellings (Assam's notified lists spell communities precisely) are rejected at document verification. Candidates claiming **SC status via tea-garden community descent** must ensure their certificate reflects the notified community name exactly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What age relaxation do SC/ST candidates get in ADRE?

Five years — Grade 3 ceiling becomes 45 and Grade 4 becomes 43 [1] [2].

### Are tea-garden communities covered under SC reservation?

Yes — Assam's SC quota extends to tea-garden community candidates holding valid SC certificates [2].

### Who issues the SC/ST certificate for ADRE?

Competent Assam authorities at district level; other states' certificates are not accepted [1] [2].

### Is there a separate exam for SC/ST candidates?

No — all candidates sit the same five-paper exam; reservation applies at the vacancy-allocation stage [1] [2].

### What if my community name is misspelled on the certificate?

Obtain a corrected certificate before applying — mismatched spellings are a common document-verification failure [1] [2].

### Do SC/ST candidates pay lower fees?

Concessions apply as per the notification; check the current fee annexure at application time [1] [2].
