The **ADRE age limit** is one of the most generous among Indian government recruitments — and one of the most misunderstood. **Grade 3 allows candidates up to 40 years old** and **Grade 4 up to 38**, both measured against the notification's cut-off date [1] [2]. Unlike SSC (which closes at 25–27) or police recruitment (which closes at 31), ADRE keeps Assam's job market open deep into a candidate's thirties. This guide explains the limits, the calculation, and the traps.

## Quick Answer: Age Limit Summary

| Grade | Minimum age | Upper age limit | As of (typical) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grade 3 | 18 years | 40 years | 1 January of the notification year |
| Grade 4 | 18 years | 38 years | 1 January of the notification year |
| Relaxations | — | SC/ST +5, OBC/MOBC +3, PwBD up to +10, ESM as per rules | Applied to the upper limit |

## How the Age Is Calculated

Every ADRE notification fixes a **reference date** — typically **1 January of the notification year** — and your age is computed against that date, not against the exam date or application date [1] [2]. The rule is binary: if you have *completed* 18 and have *not completed* 40 (or 38) on that date, you are within the limit. Practically, this means the last eligible birth cohort for a notification dated 2026 is typically born between **2 January 1986 and 1 January 2008** for Grade 3 — verify the exact dates in your notification's annexure.

## Why ADRE's Limit Matters

The 38–40 ceiling is ADRE's structural advantage over national exams: **SSC CHSL closes at 27 and MTS at 25, Assam Police constables at 31**, while ADRE Grade 3 remains open until 40 [1] [2]. For Assam's large pool of graduates in their early-to-mid thirties — career changers, self-employed, and those who missed earlier rounds — ADRE is frequently the **only government recruitment still open to them**. This is also why the exam draws applications well into the lakhs: the eligible population is simply wider.

## The Age-Proof Rule

A critical operational detail: **ADRE verifies age exclusively through the HSLC/Matriculation certificate** — Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, and birth certificates are explicitly not accepted for age proof [1] [2]. Candidates whose matriculation records show a different date of birth must regularise the discrepancy through an affidavit and gazette notification *before* applying. Name spellings must also match across the matriculation certificate, degree certificates, and Aadhaar to survive document verification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the ADRE age limit for Grade 3?

18 to 40 years, calculated against the notification's reference date (typically 1 January of the notification year) [1] [2].

### What is the ADRE age limit for Grade 4?

18 to 38 years, on the same reference-date basis [1] [2].

### Is age calculated on the exam date?

No — on the notification's fixed reference date, usually 1 January of the notification year [1] [2].

### What document proves my age?

Only the HSLC/Matriculation certificate; Aadhaar, voter ID, and birth certificates are not accepted for age proof [1] [2].

### Can I apply if I turn 40 in the exam year?

If your 40th birthday falls after the reference date, yes — the limit is fixed on the reference date, not the exam date [1] [2].

### How does ADRE compare with other exams?

Far more generous: SSC CHSL closes at 27, MTS at 25, and police constable at 31, versus ADRE's 38–40 [1] [2].
