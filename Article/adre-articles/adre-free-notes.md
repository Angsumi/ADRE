Long books are for learning; **short notes are for scoring**. In the final weeks of ADRE preparation, the candidates who revise from condensed one-page summaries per topic consistently out-score those who re-read thick books. This page organises the free ADRE notes available on this site — subject by subject — and teaches you how to build your own parallel notes so that revision becomes a 20-minute daily habit instead of a weekly burden.

The logic is simple: ADRE rewards fast recall under time pressure across 135–150 questions, and recall speed comes from material you have seen many times in small form.

## Mathematics Notes: One Page Per Chapter

The maths notes reduce every arithmetic chapter to the formulas and patterns ADRE actually tests. The core pages to revise from are the [number system](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/number-system/) essentials (divisibility rules, remainders), [percentages](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/percentages/) shortcuts, [ratio and proportion](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/ratio-and-proportion/) methods, [simple interest](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/simple-interest/) and [compound interest](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/compound-interest/) formulas, [profit and loss](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/profit-and-loss/) percentage logic, [time and work](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/word-problems-arithmetic/) unit method, [speed and distance](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/speed-distance-time/) average-speed rules, [averages](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/averages/) tricks, and [mensuration](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/mensuration/) area-volume formulas.

The discipline: after finishing a chapter from your book, compress it into a single handwritten page with at most ten formulas and two worked examples. That page — not the book — is what you revise on Day 28, Day 14, Day 3, and the morning of the exam.

## English Notes: Rules in Checklist Form

English notes work best as checklists rather than explanations, because ADRE questions test rule application. The note set follows the grammar topics: tenses and [subject-verb agreement](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/subject-verb-agreement-tenses/), [prepositions](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/prepositions/) with their fixed usages, [active and passive voice](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/active-and-passive-voice/) transformation rules, [degrees of comparison](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/degrees-of-comparison/), [pronouns](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/pronouns/), and the [error spotting](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/english-grammar-usage/) pattern list (agreement errors, article misuse, parallelism). Vocabulary notes — [synonyms](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/synonyms/), [antonyms](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/antonyms/), [idioms and phrases](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/idioms-phrases/), and [one-word substitution](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/one-word-substitution/) — are best maintained as a running list of ten new entries per day rather than memorised in bulk.

For Paper 4 candidates, add a Reading Comprehension notes page: the five most common RC question types and the elimination heuristics that save 30–40 seconds per passage.

## Reasoning Notes: One Method, One Line

Reasoning has about a dozen question families, and each family has one core method. The notes compress them: [coding-decoding](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/coding-decoding/) letter-shift logic, [number series](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/number-series/) difference patterns, [analogy](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/analogy/) relation types, [blood relations](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/blood-relations/) family-tree conventions, [direction sense](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/direction-sense-test/) orientation rules, [syllogism](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/syllogism/) Venn interpretation, [seating arrangement](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/seating-arrangement/) diagramming, and [non-verbal reasoning](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/non-verbal-reasoning/) pattern recognition. One line of method plus one worked example per family is enough; the rest of the revision is re-solving your error-log questions.

## GK and Assam GK Notes: One-Liners Win

General Knowledge notes are most effective as one-liner fact lists, because ADRE GK questions are almost always single-fact recall. Build two lists. The **India list** covers important days, books and authors, countries and capitals, world organisations, and science facts, drawn selectively from what [previous year papers](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/previous-year-question-paper/) show ADRE asking. The **Assam list** is the higher priority: Ahom dynasty kings and dates, major battles, rivers, districts, national parks and sanctuaries, Bihu and Satra culture, Assamese literature and heroes, and state schemes — all structured in the [Assam GK](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/) section, with [Assam history](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/history/) as the backbone.

Add current affairs one-liners to the Assam list every month from the [current affairs](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/current-affairs/) pages, and in the final 60 days the last six months of Assam and national one-liners become your most-revised document.

## How to Build and Use Your Notes System

The system that turns notes into marks has four rules. **First, build as you learn** — never defer note-making to "the revision phase", because rewriting a book you never condensed is impossible in one week. **Second, keep each topic to one page**: if a topic needs two pages, you have not condensed it enough. **Third, revise on a schedule** — new notes at day 3, day 10, and then weekly — which is the structure built into our [30-day](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/30-day-study-plan/), [60-day](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/60-day-study-plan/), and [90-day](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/90-day-study-plan/) study plans. **Fourth, let the error log drive the final week**: the notes you revise in the last seven days should be your formula sheets, grammar checklists, Assam one-liners, and every mistake you recorded in mock tests — nothing else.

Log your note-making progress in the [study planner](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/study-planner/) alongside your study plan so both systems advance together.

## Notes Plus Testing: The Final-Month Loop

Notes alone do not raise scores; notes plus question re-solving do. In the final month, pair every revision session with practice: revise the maths formula page, then solve ten [percentage](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/percentages/) and [profit-and-loss](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/profit-and-loss/) questions; revise the English checklist, then run an error-spotting set; revise the Assam one-liners, then attempt [history of Assam](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/history-of-assam/) questions. Anchor the loop with full [mock tests](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/mock-test/) every two to three days, scored with the [score calculator](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/score-calculator/) so your attempt strategy matures alongside your recall.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are the ADRE free notes enough for final revision?

For most candidates, yes — they cover the formula sets, grammar rules, reasoning methods, and Assam GK one-liners that carry the majority of final-week revision weight. Add your own error-log pages from mock tests to complete the set.

### When should I start making revision notes?

From the first day of study. Compress every topic you finish into one page immediately; a notes system built over 60–90 days revises itself, while notes deferred to the final week never get made.

### Which subject's notes should I revise most in the last week?

Mathematics formula sheets and English grammar checklists first, because they convert fastest into marks; then Assam GK one-liners and current affairs. Social Studies notes only for the highest-repeat topics.

### How long should a topic note be?

One page per topic — roughly ten formulas or rules and one or two worked examples. If a note exceeds one page, split the topic or cut lower-frequency content.

### Do the free notes cover Assam GK for ADRE?

Yes. The notes follow the [Assam GK](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/) structure: history, geography, culture, literature, personalities, schemes, and current affairs, with [Assam history](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/history/) as the core.

### Can I use these notes if I follow the 30-day study plan?

Yes — the 30-day plan's final week assumes exactly this notes system. Candidates on the 30-day plan should prioritise revising existing notes over making new ones, since there is no time to build a full set from scratch.
