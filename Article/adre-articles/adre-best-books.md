Choosing the **best books for ADRE preparation** is simpler than most aspirants believe. Because the ADRE syllabus sits at the school level and the exam is objective MCQ-based, you need only **one book per subject** plus our free question banks and mock tests — not a library. This page gives you a subject-wise recommendation for each section of the Grade 3 and Grade 4 papers, explains why each book fits the ADRE pattern, and shows a total budget plan under a realistic cost [1] [2].

A warning before the list: the most common ADRE preparation mistake is **resource hopping** — buying five Maths books, switching halfway, and restarting your notes. Buy one book per subject, finish it, and pair it with the topic-wise practice on this site.

## Mathematics: Why R.S. Aggarwal Wins for ADRE

The General Mathematics section carries 30–35 marks in every paper, and its ADRE topics — [number system](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/number-system/), [HCF and LCM](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/hcf-and-lcm/), [percentages](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/percentages/), [ratio and proportion](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/ratio-and-proportion/), [simple interest](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/simple-interest/), [compound interest](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/compound-interest/), [profit and loss](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/profit-and-loss/), [averages](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/averages/), [time and work](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/word-problems-arithmetic/), [speed-distance-time](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/speed-distance-time/), and [mensuration](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/mensuration/) — map almost one-to-one onto R.S. Aggarwal's chapters [1] [2].

Work chapter by chapter: read the theory once, solve the solved examples, then complete 60–80 exercises per chapter. Build your own one-page formula sheet per chapter — it becomes your week-8 revision material. For Grade 4 candidates, the Class VIII–HSLC-level chapters are sufficient; Paper 4 candidates should additionally cover basic algebra and data interpretation.

## English: Grammar Rules First, Reading Daily

Objective General English by S.P. Bakshi is the standard for government-exam English because every ADRE English topic — [prepositions](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/prepositions/), [tenses](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/grammar-tenses/), [subject-verb agreement](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/subject-verb-agreement-tenses/), [active and passive voice](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/active-and-passive-voice/), [synonyms and antonyms](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/synonyms/), [idioms and phrases](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/idioms-phrases/), [one-word substitution](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/one-word-substitution/), and [error spotting](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/english-grammar-usage/) — appears as a dedicated chapter with objective exercises [1] [2].

Two usage rules make this book work. First, finish the grammar-rule chapters before touching the question sections, since ADRE questions test rule application. Second, Paper 4 candidates must add one Reading Comprehension passage daily from any newspaper or the RC section of the book, because Reading Comprehension and English Language is worth 50 marks — the heaviest single block in the exam.

## Reasoning: Learn the Method, Then Drill Speed

Reasoning contributes 20 marks, and the question types — [coding-decoding](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/coding-decoding/), [number series](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/number-series/), [analogy](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/analogy/), [blood relations](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/blood-relations/), [direction sense](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/direction-sense-test/), [syllogism](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/syllogism/), [seating arrangement](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/seating-arrangement/), and [non-verbal reasoning](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/non-verbal-reasoning/) — are all covered with worked methods in R.S. Aggarwal's reasoning book. The exam-level difficulty is moderate, so resist the temptation to buy advanced-level (CAT-grade) reasoning books; they waste time on puzzle types ADRE never asks.

## General Knowledge and Assam GK: The Two-Volume System

Static GK needs exactly two volumes. **Lucent's General Knowledge** covers the India-wide syllabus (history, geography, polity, economics, science, books and authors, important days) in one compact book — read it selectively, chapter by chapter, paired with [previous year question papers](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/previous-year-question-paper/) so you learn which chapters ADRE actually draws from.

The second volume is an **Assam-specific GK book**, because Assam history, geography, literature, personalities, schemes, and current affairs repeat in every ADRE paper at high density. Build the Assam portion primarily from our free [Assam GK](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/) hub — especially [Assam history](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/history/), [Assam administration](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/assam-administration/), and [Assam government schemes](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/assam-government-schemes/) — and use the book only for facts the site does not cover. This combination is both free-heavy and exam-accurate, which no single printed book achieves.

## Social Studies: School Textbooks Are the Source

Social Studies is the widest section (20–30 marks) and the one where expensive reference books underperform. The exam setter's actual source material is the school curriculum, so the **NCERT and SEBA Class 6–12 textbooks** for History, Geography, Civics, and Economics remain the highest-yield investment — and many candidates already own them. Study Social Studies topic by topic using the [syllabus](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/syllabus/) sub-pages ([History](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/syllabus/history/), [Geography](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/syllabus/geography/), [Polity](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/syllabus/polity/)) and verify every topic against [previous year papers](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/previous-year-question-paper/) before going deep.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the single best book for ADRE preparation?

There is no single book because ADRE has five subjects, but if forced to choose one, pick **Quantitative Aptitude by R.S. Aggarwal** — Mathematics carries the most reliably scorable marks, and this book covers the entire ADRE Maths syllabus.

### Are NCERT books enough for the ADRE exam?

For Social Studies and General Science, yes — they are the actual source level of the exam. For Mathematics, English, and Reasoning you need one objective-practice book per subject, and for GK you need Lucent plus Assam-specific material.

### Should I buy books in Assamese or English for ADRE?

Whichever language you will write the exam in. Lucent's General Knowledge is available in Assamese, and the exam question papers come in Assamese, Bodo, Bengali, English, and Hindi [1] [2]. Studying in your exam language saves translation time on the day.

### How many books do I really need for ADRE?

Five to seven: four subject books (Maths, English, Reasoning, GK), one Assam GK source, school textbooks, and a current affairs yearly — plus the free mock tests, notes, and question banks on this site. More books slow you down.

### When should I buy the current affairs book for ADRE?

Two months before the exam. Current affairs is volatile, so an early purchase means outdated content; monthly [current affairs](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/current-affairs/) pages bridge the gap until then.

### Can I prepare for ADRE without buying any books?

Largely yes. School-level concepts, our free topic-wise questions, mock tests, previous year papers, Assam GK content, and free notes cover most of the syllabus. Books mainly add structure for Mathematics, English grammar rules, and Reasoning methods.
