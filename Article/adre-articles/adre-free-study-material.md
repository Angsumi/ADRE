## Free Mock Tests and Previous Year Papers

Full-length practice is where free material beats paid coaching most clearly. The [ADRE mock tests](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/mock-test/) simulate your exact paper — 150 questions in 180 minutes for Grade 3 papers, 135 questions in 150 minutes for Grade 4 papers — with instant scoring that respects the 0.25 negative marking per wrong one-mark question [1] [2]. Rehearsing the OMR mechanics (batch bubbling, question-flagging, skipping true guesses) inside a mock test is worth 5–10 real marks on exam day.

The [previous year question papers](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/previous-year-question-paper/) serve a different job: they reveal which topics ADRE actually repeats and at what difficulty. Use them twice — subject-wise during syllabus study, and full-length during the testing phase of your study plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is free study material enough to crack ADRE?

For most candidates, yes. The ADRE syllabus is school-level and MCQ-based, and the free material here covers syllabus, concepts, topic questions, mock tests, previous year papers, notes, and Assam GK. A small book investment for Maths, English, and Reasoning concepts (see [best books](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/best-books/)) is optional, not mandatory.

### Which free resource should I use first?

The syllabus page for your paper and one diagnostic mock test. Together they tell you exactly what to study and where you stand, which makes every later hour more targeted.

### Are the free mock tests similar to the real ADRE exam?

They replicate the real pattern: question counts, durations, sections, and the 0.25 negative marking for one-mark questions [1] [2]. The main difference is difficulty calibration, which the previous year papers help you adjust for.

### How do I use the free study material if I am working?

Use the [study planner](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/tools/study-planner/) to schedule 2.5–3 hour days: one concept topic in the morning, topic questions at lunch, and Assam GK or current affairs in the evening commute. The material is structured in small daily units that fit this pattern.

### Where do I find free Assam GK material for ADRE?

The [Assam GK](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/) hub, especially [Assam history](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/assam-gk/history/), plus topic pages like [geography of Assam](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/geography-of-assam/), [culture of Assam](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/culture-of-assam/), and [Assam personalities](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/topics/personalities-of-assam/). Monthly current affairs pages cover the dynamic portion.

### How often is the free material updated?

Syllabus and topic pages are evergreen; current affairs pages are updated monthly; mock tests and previous year papers expand as new exam rounds publish papers. Bookmark the [syllabus](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/adre/syllabus/) and [current affairs](https://Angsumi.github.io/ADRE/current-affairs/) pages for the latest versions.
